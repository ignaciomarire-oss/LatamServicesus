FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

Do you work inside our existing systems?

Yes. We work in the platforms you already use — RealPage, Yardi, QuickBooks, SAP, Microsoft 365 and others — following your approval flows and access policies.

How do you handle data security and confidentiality?

We sign NDAs, use the access levels you grant (ideally individual, role-based user accounts), keep work inside your environment whenever possible and document who does what.

What hours does your team work?

Our team is in Argentina, one to two hours ahead of U.S. Eastern Time, so we work during your business day and can join your meetings live.

How do we contract with you?

LATAM SERVICES is a U.S.-registered company, so you contract with a U.S. entity under a standard services agreement, with invoicing in U.S. dollars.

How quickly can we get started?

After a discovery call we send a proposal. Onboarding typically starts with a short knowledge-transfer period while we document your processes.
